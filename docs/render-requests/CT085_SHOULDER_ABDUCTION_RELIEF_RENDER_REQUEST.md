# CT085 Shoulder abduction relief test — realistic render request

Stable ID: ct085
Status: GENERATION_READY
User-facing title: 어깨 외전 완화 검사
English: Shoulder abduction relief test

## Visual sequence
1. 시작 자세 — patient seated, symptomatic arm relaxed at side.
2. 손을 머리 위에 — patient actively abducts the symptomatic shoulder and rests hand/forearm on the head; no forceful examiner elevation.
3. 팔 증상 감소 — the same familiar radicular arm-pain/paresthesia overlay becomes clearly weaker/shorter.

## Clinical lock
- Positive response = **relief of the familiar radicular arm symptom**, not generic shoulder comfort.
- Examiner may lightly support the elbow but does not force end-range abduction.
- Do not assign a specific root level from this sign alone.
- Interpret with neurologic screen, Spurling, distraction and ULNT.
- Negative test does not rule out cervical radiculopathy.
