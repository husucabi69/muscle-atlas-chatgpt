## Clinical interpretation lock
CCFT assesses low-load motor control/endurance of the deep cervical flexors (longus colli/capitis). It is an assessment test, not a standalone disease diagnostic test. Measurement reliability/convergent validity are supported, but discriminative validity is not sufficiently consistent to justify a universal disease-positive cutoff.

## Evidence lock
- PMID 32313944 — Measurement Properties of the Craniocervical Flexion Test: A Systematic Review (2020).
- PMID 35554598 — Reliability, Validity, and Responsiveness of the CCFT: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis (2022).
- Standard protocol shown in app: 20 mmHg baseline; 22/24/26/28/30 mmHg progressive targets; ~10-second hold per stage.
