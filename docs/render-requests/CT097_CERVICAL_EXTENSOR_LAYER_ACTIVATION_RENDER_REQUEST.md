## 3-panel visual lock

### 1. 시작 자세
- Adult patient prone on examination table.
- Forehead lightly supported.
- Shoulders relaxed.
- Cervical spine near neutral or very slight flexion.
- Clinician beside the head/upper torso, observing.

### 2. 낮은 부하 활성
- Patient performs only a small, low-load cervical extension or slight rotation.
- No large head lift and no resisted maximal extension.
- Clinician places fingertips lightly and bilaterally over posterior cervical muscle mass just lateral to the spinous processes.
- The hands observe contraction timing/symmetry; they do not push deeply or resist the motion.

### 3. 보상 / 비대칭
Show one or two functional impairment patterns:
- asymmetric posterior neck muscle activation,
- chin lift / upper-cervical hinging with excessive extension,
- obvious superficial-muscle dominance,
- early postural breakdown.
Do not mark arbitrary pain in red.

## Critical clinical correction
Surface palpation does NOT selectively identify deep semispinalis or cervical multifidus as individual muscles.
If true layer differentiation is needed, the app links to C4 posterior extensor ultrasound (usv075).
