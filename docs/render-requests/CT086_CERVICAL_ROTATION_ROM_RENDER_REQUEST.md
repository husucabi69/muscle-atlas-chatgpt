# CT086 Cervical rotation ROM assessment — realistic render request

Stable ID: ct086
Status: GENERATION_READY
User-facing title: 경추 회전 ROM 평가
English: Cervical rotation range-of-motion assessment

## Visual sequence
1. 중립 자세 — patient seated upright, shoulders relaxed, trunk fixed.
2. 좌우 회전 — patient actively rotates the head left and right while the trunk/shoulders stay still.
3. 제한 / 보상 — one rotation arc is clearly shorter or compensation appears; avoid dramatic pain theatrics.

## Clinical lock
- This is primarily an **active ROM comparison**, not forceful passive end-range rotation.
- Observe trunk rotation, shoulder motion, chin lift/flexion compensation and symptom reproduction.
- Historical ~60° rotation is a cluster item, **not a universal abnormal cutoff**.
- ROM alone does not diagnose cervical radiculopathy or identify a root level.
- Interpret with neurologic exam, Spurling, distraction and ULNT.
