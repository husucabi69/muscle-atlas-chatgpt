English: Neck flexor endurance test

## 3-panel visual lock

### 1. 시작 자세
- Adult patient supine on examination table.
- Shoulders relaxed.
- Small chin tuck / craniocervical flexion: neck looks long, chin gently drawn in.
- Head still supported on the table.
- Clinician positioned beside the patient, observing rather than applying force.

### 2. 검사 시행
- Maintain the same chin tuck.
- Lift the head only a very small distance from the table.
- This must NOT look like a sit-up, large cervical flexion, or high head lift.
- Clinician observes/times the hold; no strong manual resistance.
- Head, chin, sternum and shoulder alignment must clearly show a low controlled hold.

### 3. 기능 저하 / 보상
Show one or two clearly recognizable failure patterns:
- chin tuck is lost and chin begins to lift,
- SCM becomes visibly overactive,
- head lifts too high,
- early postural breakdown / tremor.
Do not show a disease diagnosis or arbitrary pain marking.

## Clinical interpretation lock
- Functional endurance and motor-control assessment.
- Do not use one universal seconds cutoff as disease-positive.
- Record hold time + quality of posture + compensations.
- Normal performance does not rule out radiculopathy/facet/discogenic pain/cervicogenic headache/DCM.
- Poor performance does not diagnose those disorders.
