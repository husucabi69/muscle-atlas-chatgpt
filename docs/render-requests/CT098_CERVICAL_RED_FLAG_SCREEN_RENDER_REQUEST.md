## Clinical concept
This is not a single provocative special test.
It is a safety flow:
history/risk context -> objective neurologic screen -> appropriate escalation when serious pathology is plausible.

## 3-panel visual lock

### 1. 문진 · 위험신호
- Adult patient seated in clinic with neck pain.
- Clinician interviewing the patient.
- Calm realistic interaction.
- Small restrained checklist/history cue is allowed.
- Do not diagnose a specific disease in the image.

### 2. 신경학적 선별
- Show a safe objective neurologic screen.
- Prefer one clear clinically realistic action such as upper-limb motor/reflex/hand-dexterity assessment, with clinician observing.
- No forceful cervical compression, no end-range neck rotation/extension and no manipulation.

### 3. 추가평가 우선
- Clinician pauses routine musculoskeletal provocation and explains the next evaluation step.
- Use a restrained imaging/referral cue such as reviewing a cervical MRI image on a monitor or referral document.
- Do not show a dramatic confirmed diagnosis.

## Interpretation lock
- One nonspecific red flag does not confirm cancer, infection, fracture, myelopathy or arterial dissection.
- Context + objective neurologic findings + time course determine escalation.
- Blunt trauma should use a validated decision rule such as the Canadian C-Spine Rule when its inclusion conditions are met.
- Suspected vascular pathology is NOT screened by repeatedly provoking end-range neck rotation/extension.
- DCM suspicion rises with combinations of hand dysfunction, gait change, hyperreflexia/pathologic signs and progressive neurologic deficits; imaging confirms/refutes cord compression.
