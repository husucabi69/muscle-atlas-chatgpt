English: Cervical distraction test

## Locked technique
- Patient supine, cervical spine neutral or comfortably slightly flexed.
- Examiner at head of table.
- One hand broadly supports the occiput; the other supports under the chin/mandible.
- Apply a small, gradual axial distraction force **along the patient's cervical long axis toward the head**.
- Do not add rotation, side-bending or extension.
- Positive/relief finding = the patient's familiar unilateral radicular arm pain/paresthesia decreases.

## Interpretation lock
- A relief response can support cervical radiculopathy in the appropriate history/exam context.
- It does not identify a root level or prove a specific structural cause.
- Current diagnostic-accuracy evidence is limited/low certainty; interpret with neurologic exam, Spurling and ULNT.
